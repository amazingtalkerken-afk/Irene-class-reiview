🗣️ Key Grammar Patterns

  • What have you been doing / up to?
    → I have been working a lot recently.
  • How has work been?
    → Work has been busy, and I have been tired.
  • Do you like it?
    → Yes, I do! I like it! (Remember "it"!)
  • enjoy / love + V-ing:
    → I enjoyed cutting the cake / opening gifts.

🏔️ Weekend, Job & Travel

  • nail technician / manicurist / pedicurist
  • beauty clinic (醫美診所)
  • get some work done on my lips
  • do chores (做家事) / chill at home
  • go hiking / go on a hike (去爬山)
  • sunrise (日出) / mountain peak (山頂)
  • travel abroad (出國 — no "to"!)

  • milestone birthday (里程碑生日)
  • turned 30 (滿30歲)
  • throw / hold / have a party (辦派對)
  • blow up balloons (吹氣球)
  • blow out candles (吹蠟燭)
  • open presents / gifts (拆禮物)
  • go out for dinner / go out drinking
