Here is a quick cheat-sheet of everything we practiced from Classes 3 to 7.

🗣️ Key Grammar Patterns

  • What have you been doing / up to?
    → I have been working a lot recently.
  • How has work been?
    → Work has been busy, and I have been tired.
  • Do you like it?
    → Yes, I do! I like it! (Remember "it"!)
  • Have you ever held a party?
    → No, I haven't.

🏔️ Weekend & Travel Phrases

  • beauty clinic (醫美診所)
  • I'm going to get some work done on my lips.
  • do chores (做家事) / chill at home
  • go hiking / go on a hike (去爬山/健行)
  • sunrise (日出) vs. sunset (日落)
  • travel abroad (出國) / study abroad (留學)

🎂 Birthdays & Parties

  • milestone birthday (里程碑生日)
  • turned 30 (滿30歲)
  • go out drinking / shopping (出去喝酒/逛街)
  • afterwards (之後)
  • organized events (舉辦活動)
  • awkward (尷尬) vs. annoying (很煩)

🔊 Pronunciation Practice

  • recently → RE~~zen-ly
  • massage → ma-SA~~ge
  • champagne → sham-PANE
  • "-TED" endings:
    res-TED, comple-TED, celebra-TED, compe-TED, exci-TED, accep-TED, visi-TED
